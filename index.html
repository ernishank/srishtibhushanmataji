Our Services & Initiatives

Sristhi Mangalam Foundation, Shikhar Ji Madhuban

Under the auspicious inspiration, blessings and guidance of Param Pujya Jin Dharma Prabhavika Manav Ratna 105 Shri Sristhi Bhushan Mataji, a unique food arrangement is being carried out at Tirtharaj Sammed Shikhar Ji. This arrangement is dedicated not only to Jain saints but also to hundreds of shravaks and shravikas. The Sristhi Mangalam family is blessed to serve meals to 7 to 8 sadhus daily at the chauka, and sometimes this number reaches 15–18.

Sristhi Mangalam Seva Samiti (Reg.)

    Shri Adinath Sristhi Dharma Prabhavana Samiti, Sammed Shikhar Ji

    Inspired and blessed by Mataji, this organization has been fully devoted for the past 25 years to the service of Jain Dharma, propagation of religion, and service of the Chaturvidh Sangh. The Dharma Prabhavana Samiti pioneered the chauka food arrangement for the Chaturvidh Sangh at Sonagiri and Shikhar Ji.

              About Us

              Param Pujya Aryika Ratna 105 Shri Sristhi Bhushan Mataji was born in Mungaoli, a sacred town in Madhya Pradesh. The entire town was blessed by the birth of this divine soul. She filled the home of her virtuous mother Smt. Padma and ideal householder father Shri Kapoorchand with divine radiance.
